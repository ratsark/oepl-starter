# Mission

DRAFT, awaiting approval of exact wording.

oepl-starter helps people who've been given an OpenEPaperLink kit (a
hand-built access point and a few e-paper tags) go from "what is this?" to
showing their own content, with or without a home server. It's an invitation
to tinker, not a consumer product manual. It also holds what's needed to build
more kits: AP assembly, flashing, and printable cases.
